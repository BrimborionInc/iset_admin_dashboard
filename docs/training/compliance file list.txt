COMPLIANT FILE CHECKLIST
(this is not an exhaustive list of documents that may be required as part of a client application/intervention)

	Completed Client Application (signed/dated)

	EI Verification Check (both documents must be sent together to NWAC)
1. Client Consent for EI Verification (signed/dated)
2. EI Eligibility Verification (request from Coordinator)
	Indigenous Self-Declaration (First Nations, Metis, Inuit) Form (signed/dated) and/or, two (2) reference letters from Indigenous leaders from the applicant's registered Nation and/or community;
	2 Pieces of ID (Front and Back - Status/Treaty Card + DL, Health Card, Passport, other gov't issued ID)
	Confirmation of Band Funding and/or Band Denial Letter
	Educational/Training Institution:
1. Letter of Acceptance for current year/term
2. Statement of Account (tuition, books, fees, equipment, etc.)
	Client Acknowledgement of Funding Source Form (signed/dated)
	Client Authorization for Release of Student Information Form (signed/dated)
	Client Consent for use of image, video, audio Form (signed/dated), if applicable
	ISET Financial Overview - Client to complete monthly budget for Living Allowance, if applicable
1. Verification of all Income Sources for self/spouse (last 3 paystubs; CTB statement; ROE, T4s, etc.)
2. Verification of Monthly Expense (lease/mortgage statement; 3 months of utility bills, childcare, etc.)
	Social Assistance Letter from Caseworker for Top-up, if applicable
	Client Current Resume
	Case Manager Assessment- Provide application summary, recommendation and justification
	Client Funding Agreement (signed/dated by Client and Coordinator)
	Client Monthly Attendance Form (*mandatory before receiving monthly living allowance) **
1. One form for each month of living allowance provided MUST be completed and submitted by the Client and attached to the EFT form to be submitted for payment of a monthly allowance.
2. There should be one (1) Attendance Form in the Client file for each month a living allowance is provided.
