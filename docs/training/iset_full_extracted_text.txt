Indigenous Skills and Employment Training (ISET) Orientation Training Modules 2025-2026

MODULE 1:

Contribution Agreement
In April 2019, NWAC signed a ten-year Contribution Agreement with Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC) to deliver the Indigenous Skills and Employment Training (ISET) program. This program and the current funding are scheduled to end on March 31, 2029. The Contribution Agreement that NWAC holds with ESDC is valued at approximately $33,000,000 over the term of the Agreement. There are two annual funding streams within the Contribution Agreement: Employment Insurance (EI) – for those clients eligible to be funded under the Employment Insurance Program. Consolidated Revenue Fund (CRF) – for those clients or expenses that are not eligible to be funded under EI. ESDC sets the amounts eligible under the EI Stream. Funds from the CRF stream can be reallocated to the EI stream but the reverse is not permissible.

NWAC & PTMA
NWAC - Native Women’s Association of Canada Established in 1974 (50th year in 2024) We were founded on the collective goal to enhance, promote, and foster the social, economic, cultural, and political well-being of Indigenous women and girls within their respective communities and Canadian societies. NWAC also defends the rights, delivers programming to, and amplifies the perspectives of Indigenous women in their diversities. NWAC is inclusive of First Nations—on- and off-reserve, status, non-status, and disenfranchised—Inuit, and Métis. PTMA - Provincial Territorial Membership Association Member organization that support the work of NWAC. 13 grassroots organizations in every province and territory in Canada. Currently 10 women’s organizations after SK, NL and PE were removed in October 2024. NWAC has the discretion to sign sub-agreements with a PTMA to help deliver the ISET program nationally in their region.

Background
ISET Holders are Indigenous organizations that have the designated authority through Contribution Agreements with ESDC to act on behalf of Canada in the delivery of the ISET program. There are approximately 115 regional ISET holders across Canada. The Native Women’s Association of Canada (NWAC) is one of two national ISET Holders. The Native Women’s Association of Canada (NWAC) provides employment and training support services across Canada to Indigenous women and girls in all their diversities. The National Office provides overall coordination, administration and auditing of its program in all provinces and territories to ensure compliance with all aspects of the program. Where applicable, and through sub-agreements, NWAC works in collaboration with our Provincial-Territorial Member Associations (PTMAs), to deliver the program regionally/territorially.

Mandate
The Mandate of the ISETs program is to increase Indigenous participation in the Canadian labour market. The program supports First Nations, Metis and Inuit persons with access to sustainable and meaningful employment by closing the employment and earnings gaps between Indigenous and non-Indigenous people by placing greater emphasis on training and education. The ISET program provides access to training and employment supports to Canadian Indigenous women in their diversities, including status and non-status First Nations, Metis and Inuit peoples whether residing on or off-reserve, in urban centres and in rural, remote communities. The ISET program is intended to provide financial supports such as tuition, living allowance, childcare, job starts and other resources to help eligible Indigenous women in all their diversities to reach their employment and skills training goals.

Eligibility
Canadian citizen Indigenous women in all their diversities status and non-status First Nations, Metis and Inuit peoples whether residing on or off-reserve, in urban centres and in rural, remote communities Be unemployed, under-employed, or can demonstrate an immediate risk of becoming unemployed. Active NWAC or PTMA staff (including Board members) are NOT eligible to receive funding under the ISET program or by its partner organizations.

Status / Treaty Card (Self-Declaration)
Every applicant that applies for ISET funding MUST provide a copy of their Status, Treaty or Nation card and must also sign the ‘Self-Declaration’ form. Signing only the ‘Self-Declaration’ is not sufficient. Where no documentation has be provided, the onus will be on that individual to obtain the necessary documentation to support their claim of Indigenous identity/community connection, including but not limited to two (2) letters of reference from Indigenous leadership from their Band or Nation. An individual may also provide historical information regarding lack of status, disenfranchisement and/or Indigenous Services Canada (ISC) form that verifies Indigenous identity, status or heritage.

MODULE 2:

Intervention
What is an Intervention? An Individualized Action Plan activity is developed between the Client and the Case Manager. Has a specific timeframe. Intended to help a client improve their skills and employability. Enables them to fully participate in the Canadian economy.

Intervention Research
As the Case Manager, it is your role to conduct appropriate research or assessment of the client’s career/educational or training choice which includes but is not limited to: Developing informed and realistic career plans and guiding the client towards appropriate resources; Assisting the client to understand the physical and/or mental challenges associated with different occupations; Considering career paths that are emerging or are in high demand and discussing the wage scale and ability to progress within the occupation (technology, AI, red seal trades, etc.); Ensuring the client obtains the necessary skills required (competencies) and credentials to help increase their participation in the Canadian labour force; Verifying that the program leads to viable employment opportunities in a related field; Has the potential to help close the earnings gap between Indigenous and non-Indigenous peoples.

EMPLOYABILITY DIMENSION—Career Decision
“As Assessment by the Case Manager of the skills identified by the client to make an informed career choice. In making the assessment, you are required to determine if the client has a realistic goal and the ability to research alternative occupational choices.”

EMPLOYABILITY DIMENSION—Skills Enhancement
Provides clients with the necessary skills and qualifications needed to enter and perform in a chosen occupation by enrolling them in the appropriate training or education program.

Client Resume
Client MUST provide a current resume with their ISET application. As the case manager, you can use the resume both as a verification tool and a conversation starter for career exploration and planning.

Multiple Interventions
Multiple interventions may be available to clients when: each supports the needs assessment; labour market conditions prevent suitable employment; or additional training would improve employment potential.

Funding Stream (ITP)
The Individual Training Purchase provides clients with the necessary skills and qualifications to enter and perform within a chosen occupation. Provides financial assistance for education, training, related program expenses and living expenses.

Funding Stream (TWS)
Targeted Wage Subsidy encourages employers to hire individuals for a specific period, with the objective of providing on-the-job training.

MODULE 3:

Regional ISET Manager / Case Manager
Coordinates service delivery, develops Workplan, processes applications, provides case management, employer partnerships, outreach, awareness, and ensures compliance with Data Management Protocols.

Regional Workplan
Key priorities, strategies, tracking, employer engagement, events, partners, budgeting, forecasting, reporting, barriers, mitigation.

Client Application & ARMS
Create file, upload docs, record interactions, assess needs, set goals.

Case Manager Responsibilities
Ongoing communication, trauma-informed practice, progress tracking, problem-solving, referrals, follow-up, file closure.

MODULE 4:

EI and LMDA
Clients must sign EI Consent. Verification required before processing. 2–3 business days turnaround.

ARMS Requirements
Immediate creation, uploads, notes, interactions. Contact clients within 5 days. Three attempts for missing info. Closure if unresponsive.

Application Review
Three-week processing after all docs received. Thorough review including employability dimensions.

Compliance
Files must pass audit. Maintain secure handling of all client info.

MODULE 5:

Pending Applications
Application is pending until all documentation is provided, Case Manager recommendation complete, NWAC approval received, and Funding Agreement signed.

Identification Requirements
Two pieces of ID, one MUST be Status/Treaty card or two Nation letters.

Consents & Authorizations
Review funding acknowledgement, advise about consequences of false statements.

CRA
T4A issued for ALL funding. Client must report income. Tax exemption determined by CRA only.

MODULE 6:

Monthly Budget Requirements
Client must complete Financial Overview and provide verification for all income and expenses.

Income Verification
Paystubs, partner income, child benefits, disability verification, alimony/child support proof, social assistance letters, other income sources, T4s, bank statements, ROEs.

Expenses Verification
Childcare under 12 or disabled dependents; rent/mortgage; utilities; transportation (public transit or vehicle km calculation and insurance).

MODULE 7:

Living Allowance
No minimum rate. Based on needs assessment. Not intended to cover all expenses. No backdating.

Eligibility
Must disclose all income. EI claimants not eligible until completed. Attendance Report required every month.

Eligible Expenses
Rent/mortgage, basic utilities, groceries, transportation (not car payments), childcare (if increased due to training).

Attendance Report Rules
False statements may trigger repayment. Absences must be reported and sometimes require medical documentation.

MODULE 8:

Eligible Program/Employment Expenses
Tuition at accredited institutions, essential skills, upgrading, recognized programs, counselling, job search, self-employment services, certain program-related expenses.

Band/Treaty Letters
Clients MUST apply to their Band first. Denial must be on letterhead and match program request. Funding letters affect ISET eligibility.

Letter of Acceptance
Must be on official letterhead, current, matching program name. Screenshots not accepted.

Statement of Fees
Must be official, current, and match request.

Tuition Payment & Reimbursement
Paid directly to institution when possible. Reimbursements require original receipts.

Specialized Equipment
Must provide institution letter. Quotes required. Anything above $650 requires NWAC approval. Receipts required within 2 weeks.

Persons with Disabilities / Dependent Care
Supports available, case-by-case, no cap.

MODULE 9:

Case Manager Assessment
Overview must summarize client application, history, goals. All information must be consistent. Include prior interventions.

Recommendation
Include justification, research, alternatives. Case Manager not required to recommend funding.

Approval
NWAC responds within 5 days: approve, deny, meet, request resubmission. Case Manager informs client.

Client Funding Agreement
Must match approved amounts exactly. Must be reviewed and signed.

MODULE 10:

Reporting & Financial Oversight
Maintain Active Client Spreadsheet. Submit every 90 days. Track expenses, advances, outcomes. Narrative/statistical reports required.

Minimum Service Level Report
Track ALL inquiries that do not incur cost (applications, website referrals, job search access, labour market info, referrals).

Authorization to Quit (ATQ)
Allows EI claimants to leave employment to attend training under S25 authority. Not retroactive. High responsibility. Requires action plan and documentation.

ARMS Compliance
ALL applications must be entered, funded or not. Audits performed. Monthly report to NWAC Board.
